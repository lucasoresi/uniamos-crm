GET /leads

Obtiene todos los leads del usuario actual

POST /leads

Crea un nuevo lead
curl -X POST "...rest/v1/leads" -H "Authorization: Bearer KEY" -d '{"id":"...","data":{...}}'

PATCH /leads

Actualiza un lead existente
curl -X PATCH "...rest/v1/leads?id=eq.{id}" -H "Authorization: Bearer KEY" -d '{"data":{...}}'

DELETE /leads

Elimina un lead por ID
curl -X DELETE "...rest/v1/leads?id=eq.{id}" -H "Authorization: Bearer KEY"

GET /prospects
